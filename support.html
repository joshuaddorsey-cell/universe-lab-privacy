Universe Lab Support

Universe Lab is a science reader and interactive physics playground developed by Joshua Dorsey.

Contact the developer

For help, bug reports, accessibility problems, or feature requests, use the Universe Lab support form on GitHub. A GitHub account is required to submit a request. You can also read existing support requests.

Support requests are public. Include the app version, your device model, Android or iOS version, and the steps that caused the problem. Do not include passwords, contact details, payment information, or other personal information.

News is not loading

Check your internet connection, then refresh Today. Publishers can be temporarily unavailable independently. Lessons and physics labs work offline. Saved stories are links; opening the publisher's full article requires internet access.

Bookmarks and local data

Use the bookmark control on a story to save or remove its link. Bookmarks are stored on your device, without an app account.

Privacy

Read the Universe Lab privacy policy. Universe Lab has no advertising SDK or analytics.
