WeFind Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026. Developer: Joshua Dorsey.

WeFind helps people choose places near a driving-time midpoint or a selected map pin. It has no app account, advertising, advertising identifier, analytics service, or developer-operated database of your searches. We do not sell personal information.

Location and searches

If you grant foreground location permission, WeFind uses your device's latitude and longitude to find nearby places and position the map. Permission is optional: you can enter starting points, search a city/address, or place a pin manually. WeFind does not request background location.

Place names, entered addresses and selected coordinates are sent by encrypted HTTPS connections to Photon's public search service, operated by komoot. Start/end coordinates are sent to FOSSGIS's public OSRM routing service to calculate a road route and estimated travel-time midpoint. The viewed map area and technical request information are sent to OpenStreetMap's tile servers. These services receive ordinary network information, including your IP address and the app's user-agent, and may retain request logs under their own policies. WeFind does not attach an app account identifier or advertising identifier.

Device storage

Starting points and place/route results are cached in app memory for the current session, with service-result cache expiry after 15 minutes. Map images are cached on your device to reduce downloads and comply with map-provider caching rules. The developer does not receive these caches. Close the app to end the session; clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove local cached map files. System backups may retain local app files according to your phone settings.

External links

When you choose reviews, directions, or a broader area search, WeFind opens Google Maps or its website with the selected place or area in the link. This does not use the Google Places API. Your interaction with the external service is governed by Google's privacy policy. The app does not import your Google reviews or Google account information.

Choices and retention

You can deny or revoke location access in device settings, use public meeting points instead of private addresses, and choose whether to open external links. The developer has no server-side search records or app accounts to delete. For third-party request logs, contact the relevant service operator; their retention practices are outside the developer's control.

Audience and changes

WeFind is a general-purpose place finder, not a service directed to children. Its Drinks category can show bars and pubs. Free map data can be incomplete, and local business corrections may be bundled from verified public information. We update this policy when the app's practices change.
