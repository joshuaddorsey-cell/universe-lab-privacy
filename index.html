Universe Lab Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026. Universe Lab is developed by Joshua Dorsey.

Data on your device

Universe Lab has no user accounts, advertising SDKs, or analytics. The app stores links you bookmark on your device. You can remove individual bookmarks in the app or clear its stored data in Android Settings to remove all bookmarks. The developer does not receive or retain your bookmarks.

News feeds and external websites

To display news, the app requests public RSS feeds over HTTPS from NASA and ScienceDaily. Those publishers receive normal connection information such as your IP address. Universe Lab does not attach your bookmarks, account information, or device identifiers to feed requests. When you open an article or source link, your browser connects to the publisher under that site's privacy policy.

Privacy questions

Contact the developer through the Universe Lab privacy support page. Issues are public, so please omit personal information.
