Atlanta Stories Support

Browse offline history cards, filter or search, and save places locally. Use Any distance or choose a landmark if location is unavailable. Source links and live Wikipedia discovery require internet. History-pending cards are clearly identified.

Contact the developer

For help, accessibility issues or bug reports, use the developer support form. A GitHub account is required. Include the app name, version, device model, OS version and steps to reproduce the issue. Requests are public; do not include personal information, passwords or payment details.

Privacy

Read the Atlanta Stories privacy policy. No app account is needed.
