Dream Voyages Privacy Policy

Dream Voyages has no accounts, ads, analytics, or developer-operated servers. The Android release does not request Internet access. The developer does not collect or share personal data from the app.

The app stores your selected world, sound and narration levels, timer and settling preferences, collectible names, and the date of your last gift on your device. The app does not track sleep or record microphone audio. To delete these preferences and progress, clear the app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Soundscapes and recorded stories are bundled with the app. The optional spoken wind-down uses an installed offline Android text-to-speech voice. Voice downloads and engine settings are managed by Android and the engine provider.

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support page. Issues are public, so please omit personal information.
