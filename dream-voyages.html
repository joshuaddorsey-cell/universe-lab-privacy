Dream Voyages Privacy Policy

Dream Voyages has no accounts, ads, analytics, or developer-operated servers. The Android release does not request Internet access. The developer does not collect or share personal data from the app.

The app stores your selected world, sound and narration levels, timer and settling preferences, collectible names, and the date of your last gift on your device. The app does not track sleep or record microphone audio. To delete these preferences and progress, clear app storage where supported or delete the app. iPhone preferences may be included in your system backups, governed by your Apple backup settings.

Soundscapes and human-read stories are bundled with the app. On Android, the optional spoken wind-down uses an installed offline Android text-to-speech voice. The iPhone version uses Apple's system-managed speech voice. Voice availability, downloads and operating-system backup settings are controlled by the device platform. Dream Voyages does not send story text or personal data to a developer-operated service.

Updated November 19, 2026, to describe the iPhone version in preparation as well as the existing Android edition.

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support page. Issues are public, so please omit personal information.
