WeFind Support

Find places near a driving-time halfway point, near your phone, or around a map pin. No app account or payment is required.

Meet halfway

Enter and select both starting points. City and county results are labeled separately. Choose Eat, Coffee, Drinks or Fun and a 1, 5, 10 or 20-mile search radius. WeFind estimates the time midpoint of a road route and finds places around that point. Distances on place cards are straight-line distances from the search center. Actual drives to a chosen venue can differ; estimates do not include live traffic.

Drop a pin

Search for a city/address/place and select the intended result, use your phone's location, or pan/tap the map to position the heart tip. Set the category and radius, then search. The pin can be positioned anywhere supported by the map; available place listings vary by region. If a category has no mapped venues, try another category or radius, or choose the external Google Maps search.

Missing or duplicate places

Free OpenStreetMap-based data can be incomplete or outdated. Nearby duplicate records are merged where identifiable. A small set of verified local corrections is included. The app does not provide embedded Google ratings; choose Reviews & directions to open Google Maps. Check current opening hours and venue details before traveling.

Troubleshooting

If a free service is busy, check your connection and retry later. Location permission is optional; use a named starting point or map pin if permission is denied. There is no paid-service fallback.
