Atlanta Stories Privacy Policy

Atlanta Stories uses your device location, if you grant permission, to show nearby places and calculate distances. The app keeps the latest location in memory while you use it and does not store your location on disk. You may revoke location access in device settings, choose a landmark, or browse at Any distance.

If you choose live Wikipedia discovery, the app sends your typed search to English Wikipedia. If you search for nearby entries without entering text, the app sends coordinates rounded to three decimal places. Wikipedia receives standard connection information. Links to maps, articles, and research sites open external services, where their own privacy policies apply.

Saved places are stored on your device. You can remove them in the app or delete the app's local data. On iPhone, stored app data may be included in system backups under your Apple backup settings. Android narration uses your configured text-to-speech engine; the iPhone version uses Apple's system speech voice. These platform services manage voice availability and downloads under their own settings and policies.

Updated November 19, 2026, to describe the iPhone version in preparation as well as the existing Android edition.

The developer does not operate an account system or receive your location or search history. For privacy questions, use the developer support page. Issues are public, so please omit personal information.
